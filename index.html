The Procrastination Loop

Task
Bad feeling
Delay
Relief
How I feel now > My goals for later

Task leads to a bad feeling. The bad feeling leads to delay. Delay brings relief. Relief leads back to the task.

Procrastination is about feelings, not laziness. A task gives you a bad feeling, so you delay to feel better now. The relief makes you want to delay again next time. (Sirois & Pychyl, 2013)

Name the feeling. What do you feel right before you put it off?
